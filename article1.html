TOP 5 beach destinations for 2026

A great beach is more than sand and water. It is the moment you step out of the car, hear the waves before you see them and forget what time it is. The beaches on this list were chosen for exactly that feeling: clear water, soft light and enough space to breathe, whether you come for snorkeling, long walks or doing absolutely nothing.

Before you book, it helps to keep a few things in mind:

  1. Timing matters most. Visit in the shoulder season, when the weather is still good but crowds and prices are lower.
  2. Check the conditions before you swim. Tides, currents and local warning flags tell you more than any guidebook.
  3. Pack reef-safe sunscreen, a light cover-up and a reusable water bottle.
  4. Leave the beach as you found it and take all of your rubbish with you.
Pro tipps
  1. Arrive early in the morning. The light is softer, the sand is empty and the best spots are still free. Useful link. A short walk away from the main entrance is often enough to find a quieter corner.
  2. Bring your own mask and snorkel if you can. Rental gear on busy beaches is often expensive, and your own fits better.

Whichever beach you choose, give it time. The best moments rarely happen on a schedule: a sudden swim at sunset, a chat with a local fisherman or a picnic on warm rocks. Plan the trip, but leave room for those unplanned hours.

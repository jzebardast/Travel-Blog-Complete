Copenhagen in 3 Days: Big Experiences, Smart Spending

Discover Copenhagen, where historic charm meets modern design, vibrant cycling culture and waterfront living. Experience the heart of Scandinavia through cozy cafés, innovative cuisine, and effortlessly cool city life.

Copenhagen is small enough to explore in three days and rich enough to fill them. Colorful houses line the canals, bicycles outnumber cars and almost every corner has a café worth sitting in. This guide shows how to see the highlights without spending more than you need to.

Start your first day at Nyhavn and walk along the harbor towards the Little Mermaid. Spend the afternoon in the old town with its squares, churches and small shops. On day two, visit Rosenborg Castle and the green parks around it, then have lunch at the food stalls of Torvehallerne. Keep the third day relaxed: rent a bike, cross the harbor bridges and end the evening by the water.

A few ways to save money without missing out:

  • Use a city pass if you plan to visit several attractions, and compare it with single tickets first.
  • Eat at markets and bakeries at lunchtime. It is cheaper than dinner and just as local.
  • Get around by bike or on foot. The center is compact and most sights are close together.

Copenhagen rewards slow travel. Sit down for a coffee by the canal, watch the cyclists pass and let the city set the pace. Some of the best moments cost nothing at all: a sunset at the harbor, a walk through a quiet park or a swim in one of the harbor baths in summer.

Pro tipps
  1. Book popular attractions online in advance to skip the line. Useful link. Many places also offer a lower price for tickets bought early.
  2. Carry a reusable water bottle. Tap water in Copenhagen is clean and free, and you can refill your bottle almost everywhere.
